Fair questions

The things people ask before saying yes.

Why would you build it before I pay?

Because asking a stranger on the internet for money upfront is a bad deal for you, and I'd rather show you the work than describe it. It costs me time, not money, so I can afford to lose the ones that don't work out.

Can it be hacked?

There's no database, no login page and no admin panel on it — which is what usually gets broken into on a normal website. It's just a page being served, over a secure connection. It's a good deal safer than a typical WordPress site.

Will it stop working later?

There's nothing to expire. Once it's on your own account it stays up with no renewal and no monthly fee. I'm not going to promise you "forever" because nobody honestly can — but there's no bill coming and no switch I can flip.

What if I want changes after paying?

Text changes, price updates, new items — send them across and I'll do them. If you want to do it yourself later, the files are plain and readable.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You need a free GitHub account at the end so the page sits under your name, and I'll walk you through making one. That's the whole technical requirement.

Can I have more than one page?

These are deliberately one page — that's what works for a business where people just want your prices, your hours and a way to message you. If you need something bigger, tell me what and I'll quote it honestly or tell you I'm not the right person.
