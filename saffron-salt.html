About

Everything is baked the morning it reaches you.

We started in 2021 out of a home kitchen and never moved to bulk production. Every order is made fresh on the day, in quantities small enough that we can still taste-check each batch. No preservatives, no ready-made mixes, no last-week stock.

🥣

Made to order
