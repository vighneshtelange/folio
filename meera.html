Freelance web designer and front-end developer — I build the site, not the mockup of the site

Bengaluru · works with clients anywhere

I design small commercial sites and then build them myself, which means nothing gets lost in the handover and nobody has to explain to a developer what the spacing was supposed to do.

Most of my work is for founders spending their own money, so I care about the boring things: how fast it loads on a mid-range Android on mobile data, whether the checkout works with one thumb, and whether they can update the prices themselves without calling me.

A single-page ordering site for a two-branch restaurant that was paying 28% commission to an aggregator. Orders land in a WhatsApp thread the kitchen already watches, so nobody had to learn new software.
